# Eval decision template

Copy the two blocks below into `evals/decisions/<slug>/BRIEF.md` and `evals/decisions/<slug>/KEY.md`. Write the brief first, from the record as it stood on the decision date, and only then open the key; a brief written with the outcome in mind is the commonest way an eval decision becomes useless.

---

## BRIEF.md

```
# <decision, in one line, as it was put at the time>

As of: <YYYY-MM-DD>            # nothing below is dated after this day
Owner: <who decided>
Deadline: <when it had to be decided, or "none stated">
Stakes: <money, reversibility, who else it touched — as known then>

## The decision as stated
<the question as the owner put it, verbatim where possible, including the option they were leaning to>

## The record as it stood
<the facts that were available: numbers with their sources and dates, the governing document or rule in force, what the live record showed, earlier decisions of this kind and how they had gone — each marked measured / estimated / inferred as the skill would mark it>

## What was not known
<the open questions at the time, as they were understood then>

## Constraints and rules in force
<budget, policy, approvals, anything the pass must check against>
```

## KEY.md

```
# Key — <slug>          # sealed: never in the context that runs the pass

Decided on: <YYYY-MM-DD>
Chosen: <what was done>
Alternatives considered at the time: <list, or "none — one option">

## What happened
Observable: <the number, date or yes/no that settled it, with its source>
Resolved on: <YYYY-MM-DD>
Verdict: <right call | wrong call | right call, wrong first step | undecidable — and why>

## What broke, if anything
Cause: <the thing that actually went wrong, or "nothing">
Would a kill criterion have caught it: <what signal, by when>
The link that failed: <the assumption that turned out false>

## In hindsight
Would decide again: <yes | no | yes, but staged>
What the owner would change: <one line>
Notes for the scorer: <anything that makes scoring this decision tricky>
```
